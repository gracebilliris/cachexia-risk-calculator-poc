Research-only synthetic-data demonstrator

Cachexia and pre-cachexia simulation prototype

Not for clinical use. Patients and outputs are synthetic. Risk relationships are simulation assumptions, not clinically validated effects. Do not use this prototype for diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, patient care, or any medical decision.

Baseline inputs

Unknown remains distinct from “no”. Required values are marked *.

Dated weight history

Only dates on or before prediction are predictors.

Baseline-derived variables

Implemented cachexia criteria met?Not calculated
Provisional early-risk pattern met?Not calculated

Implemented cachexia criteria: >5% weight loss, >2% loss with BMI <20 kg/m², or >2% loss with explicitly documented sarcopenia. For sarcopenia, “no” means assessed and absent; “unknown” means not assessed or not documented. Sarcopenia is never inferred and affects the criteria label only; no simulated risk coefficient has been assigned. Provisional early-risk pattern: cachexia criteria not met, >1% to ≤5% loss, and reduced appetite=yes. This project proposal requires clinical-reviewer and clinical-reviewer’s review.
